Public Holiday Calendars

한국어 English 日本語

Subscribe in your calendar app and public holidays for South Korea, Japan, and Germany arrive automatically. Korean dates are derived from statute and astronomical calculation, then checked against the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute's special-day data. Japanese dates follow 内閣府 「国民の祝日について」. German feeds cover the nationwide set plus per-state feeds that add holidays under state law. Every entry carries its source.

Subscribe

Entries in the Japanese feed are named in the original Japanese (元日, 休日（元日）, and so on). Entries in the German feeds use the wording of the statute (Neujahr, 1. Mai, and so on).

In Google Calendar use “From URL”; in Apple Calendar use “New Calendar Subscription” and paste the address above.

Source checking

Every date is checked against our own calculation and the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute's special-day data. Beyond that, checking the statute and gazette text is counted per entry in the rule table (a holiday definition and its provision); the number below is how many of the 48 entries have not been checked yet.

The two do not always agree. 14 August 2015 — a temporary holiday marking 70 years since liberation — is absent from the special-day API. It was confirmed from the 4 August 2015 cabinet resolution and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs announcement, and included.

The rule tables, sources, and generating code are all public — github.com/lunalism/holidays
